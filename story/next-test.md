# Continuing on its own

This page carries `next: next-test-2` in its front matter, and that is the whole exit: there is no menu here, because a live `next` wins over choices - even a page that lists a full menu never shows it while its continuation is live

Click anywhere (or press Space) to move on; the story appends the next page without asking you to pick anything

The next page pairs `next` with `if:`, which turns the continuation into a conditional one: while the condition is false the page falls through to its choices as normal
